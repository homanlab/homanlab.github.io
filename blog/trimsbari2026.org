*Treatment resistance indicators from mechanisms in schizophrenia (TRIMS)*

The [[https://data.snf.ch/grants/grant/10009051][Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF)]] has awarded 2.5 million
Swiss francs to our new multinational TRIMS project led by the
University of Zurich ([[https://homanlab.github.io/philipp][Philipp]]), in collaboration with the Universities
of Bari ([[https://www.libd.org/team/giulio-pergola-phd/][Prof. Giulio Pergola]]) and University of Applied Sciences of
Western Switzerland Fribourg ([[https://people.hes-so.ch/de/profile/54465174-elena-mugellini][Elena Mugellini]]). The project aims to
understand why some people with schizophrenia do not respond to
standard medications, and when switching to clozapine is warranted.

This decision is currently often made too late, with significant
consequences for patients. To address this, the TRIMS consortium will
develop a data-driven decision support system to help clinicians
identify the right moment for a switch to clozapine. The goal is to
reduce unnecessary exposure to medications that do not help, thereby
improving care for people with schizophrenia.

The project was officially started in Bari last week, where the TRIMS
consortium had the pleasure to meet in person and enjoy the
hospitality of the Bari team. For more information on the project,
make sure to visit our project website [[https://trims.uzh.ch][https://trims.uzh.ch]]!
