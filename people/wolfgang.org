Wolfgang Omlor is a board-certified psychiatrist and attending
physician in adult psychiatry at the Psychiatric University Hospital
Zurich. He obtained his medical doctorate summa cum laude at the
University of Freiburg under the supervision of Prof. Rumyana Kristeva
and completed his PhD in natural sciences at the University of Zurich
with Prof. Fritjof Helmchen. As a fellow of the German Academic
Scholarship Foundation, he undertook clinical study periods at Harvard
Medical School and the University of Oxford. His clinical training
included neurology at the University Hospital Zurich and adult
psychiatry at the PUK, where he is also a postdoctoral researcher in
the Homan Lab. His previous research focused on corticospinal
interactions and neural circuit dynamics in the mammalian motor
system, using electrophysiology, optogenetics and calcium imaging. His
current research bridges systems neuroscience and psychiatry, with a
focus on neurocomputational and multimodal phenotyping of
schizophrenia-spectrum disorders. He has published in leading
peer-reviewed journals including The American Journal of Psychiatry,
Nature Communications, Science and The Journal of Neuroscience,
obtained competitive external research funding, serves as a reviewer
for international scientific journals and teaches neurophysiology and
psychiatry.
