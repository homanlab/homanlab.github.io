Paula studied medicine at the University of Göttingen, Germany. A
fellowship from the Else Kröner Graduate School for Medical Students
allowed her to take a year out for her doctoral thesis in the Division
of Molecular Psychiatry, where she studied behaviour and brain
pathology in an Alzheimer's disease mouse model. This sparked her
interest in psychiatry and translational research. After a year in
neurology, she joined the University Hospital of Psychiatry Zurich as
a physician in 2025. In the Homan Lab, she now investigates brain and
retinal imaging in patients with psychiatric disorders.
